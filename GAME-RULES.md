# The sixteen games

## 1. Lumen Loom

Precision · 75 sec / untimed · modes: rush, practice

1. Drag around blue circles and gold diamonds. Release to close the loop.
2. Every clean seed earns 10. Equal, nonzero blue and gold counts earn Harmony ×2, rising to ×6 across consecutive balanced harvests.
3. A coral thorn cancels that harvest, resets Harmony and costs 3 seconds in Rush. Empty loops cost nothing.

Controls: Drag · keyboard: arrows move pen, Space draws, Enter closes

## 2. Orbit Echo

Memory · 2 min · modes: stages, practice

1. Watch the numbered symbols flash. The ring then visibly rotates by the stated number of positions.
2. Repeat the sequence at its new positions. From stage 5, expert mode reflects positions across the vertical axis before rotating; the rule is shown before each round.
3. Two mistakes end the run. A missed round reveals its answer. Replay marks the run assisted.

Controls: Click ring positions · keys 1–8

## 3. Relay Mosaic

Logic · 2–4 min · modes: puzzle

1. Click a tile to cycle dark · blue circle · gold diamond. Its arrow neighbor cycles too.
2. Match every tile in the target. Hover or focus a tile to preview both affected tiles.
3. Undo is always available. Hint highlights a valid next move and reduces rewards to 25%.

Controls: Click or Tab + Enter · hover/focus previews effects

## 4. Twin Current

Planning · 2–4 min · modes: puzzle

1. Each direction moves both tokens. The right board rotates your direction clockwise by the shown amount.
2. A wall blocks only the token facing it. Both tokens must reach their goals at the same time.
3. Plan ahead, undo freely, or ask for an assisted hint.

Controls: Arrows / WASD · on-screen direction pad

## 5. Pulse Ledger

Logic · 2–4 min · modes: puzzle

1. Match all three exact energy targets using the available operation cards. Each card can be used once.
2. Transfer moves a stated amount; swap exchanges two containers. Split moves exactly half of an even-valued source into another container.
3. Preview a card before using it. Values never go negative and odd values cannot split.

Controls: Choose a card · confirm its exact effect

## 6. Lantern Route

Planning · 2–3 min · modes: puzzle

1. Reach the lighthouse after collecting the key. Every move or wait advances the tide.
2. Tidal tiles are passable only at low tide (phase 0). Solid docks are always safe.
3. The next tide is shown. Waiting can open a route; it also counts as a move.

Controls: Arrows / WASD · wait button · touch pad

## 7. Parcel Fold

Spatial · 2–4 min · modes: puzzle

1. Cover the 4 × 4 parcel with the eight numbered dominoes. No overlaps and no gaps.
2. Choose a piece, rotate it, then choose its top-left square. Some squares require a particular piece number.
3. Use the anchors to plan the packing. Undo removes your previous placement.

Controls: Select piece · Rotate · click a square

## 8. Cipher Garden

Logic · 1–2 min · modes: deduction, practice

1. One of eight specimens is hidden. Each has three visible features: leaf shape, petals, and stem.
2. Spend up to three tests to reveal whether the hidden specimen has a chosen feature. Eliminate candidates using the evidence.
3. Select the matching specimen. A wrong guess ends the run. Fewer tests earn more performance points.

Controls: Choose feature tests · choose a specimen

## 9. Signal Sort

Reaction · 60 sec / untimed · modes: shift, practice

1. Route each package into one of three bins using the rule above it: shape, stripe count, or number.
2. The rule changes every five packages and is announced before you continue. Read it before acting.
3. Finish 30 packages. At least 70% correct completes the shift. Untimed practice uses the same decisions.

Controls: Bins 1–3 · keyboard 1 / 2 / 3

## 10. Gravity Ferry

Planning · 2–3 min · modes: puzzle

1. Each turn, thrust changes velocity by −1, 0, or +1, then the ferry moves by that new velocity.
2. Dock exactly on the destination with velocity zero. Speed is limited to three cells per turn.
3. Avoid leaving the channel. Every turn uses fuel; undo or restart if your approach goes wrong.

Controls: Left / Right thrust · Coast · touch controls

## 11. Mirror Harbor

Spatial · 1–3 min · modes: puzzle

1. A beam enters from the left. Mirrors reflect it by 90 degrees. Click a mirror to switch / and \.
2. Guide the beam to the gold receiver on the right. The live beam preview shows every reflection.
3. Find the shortest set of turns, or use an assisted hint.

Controls: Click a mirror · Tab + Enter

## 12. Tempo Thread

Rhythm · 25 sec / untimed · modes: rhythm, practice

1. Three lanes share a beat line. Tap the marked lane when a bright note reaches the line.
2. Hollow notes are rests: let them pass. Each note is judged once. Matching at least 70% completes the thread.
3. Practice has no timer: decide whether to tap a lane or skip each rest.

Controls: Keys 1–3 or lane buttons · Practice has Skip rest

## 13. Comet Catch

Precision · 30 sec / untimed · modes: chase, practice

1. Catch the bright numbered comet. It moves along a known orbit; aim inside its ring.
2. Each comet can be collected once. At least eight of twelve completes the run. Misses reduce accuracy.
3. Practice freezes each comet and removes the deadline. Keyboard access uses a movable targeting cursor.

Controls: Click / tap target · arrows aim · Enter catches

## 14. Stillwater

Relaxed · Untimed · modes: flow, practice

1. Trace from the glowing checkpoint to the next numbered checkpoint, then release.
2. Keep every part of your line clear of coral stones. A collision returns you to the current checkpoint.
3. Connect all six checkpoints. There is no time limit; shorter, clean traces earn more.

Controls: Drag checkpoint to checkpoint · arrows / Space / Enter

## 15. Switchyard

Planning · 2–4 min · modes: puzzle

1. Move the top numbered car from one siding to another. Each siding holds at most three cars.
2. Match all three target stacks exactly, from bottom to top. Use the spare space to reverse an order.
3. Choose a source then a destination. Undo and assisted hints are available.

Controls: Select source siding · select destination siding

## 16. Recall Atelier

Memory · 1–2 min · modes: memory, practice

1. Study the arrangement of symbols, then hide it when you are ready. Expert mode gives a shorter viewing window.
2. Choose each symbol and place it on the remembered square. You can move a placed symbol before checking.
3. Check once to finish. Replaying the arrangement marks the run assisted.

Controls: Choose a symbol · choose a square · Check arrangement
